## 🎯 Objectives

- Represent the RoboCar chassis using orthographic & isometric projections.
- Create an optimized 3D CAD model integrating sustainable design.
- Analyze structural strength using sectional views.
- Align design decisions with UN Sustainable Development Goals.

## 🧩 Modules Covered

1. Projection of Points and Lines
2. Section of Solids
3. Orthographic and Sectional Orthographic Projections
4. Isometric Projections and 3D Modeling

## 🌍 Sustainable Development Goals (SDGs) Integrated

### SDG 9 — Industry, Innovation & Infrastructure
- Use of CAD tools promotes digital innovation.
- Design optimized for additive manufacturing.
- Simulation and digital assembly reduce prototype failures.
- Encourages modern engineering practices in education.

### SDG 12 — Responsible Consumption & Production
- Virtual prototyping reduces physical waste.
- Ribbed structure uses ~40% less material while maintaining strength.
- PLA recommended as a biodegradable material.
- Design is easily modifiable, promoting reusability.

## 🛠️ Methodology

**Tool Used:** AutoCAD 2024 — for 2D views, sections, dimensioning, and 3D modeling.

**Techniques Applied:**
- Orthographic Projection (Front, Top, Side views)
- Sectional Views (ribbed structure, material optimization)
- Isometric 3D Modeling
- Solid operations: Extrude, Presspull, Fillet, Subtract
- Dimensioning using standard engineering conventions
- Layer management for line types (center, hidden, visible)

---

## 📐 Design Iteration — Dimension Comparison

The chassis went through two design stages, with the final design significantly more compact and material-efficient.

| Dimension | Stage 1 | Stage 2 (Final) |
|---|---|---|
| Overall Length | 416 mm | 325 mm |
| Wheelbase | 224 mm | 224 mm |
| Ground Clearance | 18 mm | 18 mm |
| Side Panel Height | 51/52 mm | 56 mm |
| Wheel Radius | 20 mm | 28 mm |
| Wheel Width | 10 mm | 25 mm |

### How 3D Visualization Improved Design Accuracy

**Better Structural Understanding**
- Sectional views revealed internal ribbing → ~40% material saved while keeping strength.
- Identification of weak zones and overbuilt regions.

**Component Fit Verification**
- Digital assembly of motor mounts, battery compartments, and sensor spaces.
- Accurate hole alignment using subtract, fillet, and presspull commands.

**Error Reduction Before Manufacturing**
- Avoids trial-and-error physical prototypes → supports SDG 12 (Reduced Waste).
- Ensures 3D printability with no unnecessary support structures.

## 🧪 Materials Considered

| Material | Notes |
|---|---|
| **PLA (Polylactic Acid)** ✅ Selected | Lightweight, biodegradable, ideal for 3D printing & educational prototyping. Aligns with SDG 12. |
| **ABS Plastic** | Higher strength and heat resistance; suitable for rugged RoboRace applications. |
| **Aluminium** | Not selected — referenced in literature as stable and strong, but less sustainable for small-scale student prototyping. |

**Reason for PLA Preference:**
- Easy to print
- Less brittle when a ribbed design is used
- Lower environmental impact
- Good surface finish

**Final Achievements**
- Accurate 2D and 3D representation of the chassis

- Dimensions optimized for weight, stability, and print feasibility
- Improved design accuracy through 3D visualization

**Performance Interpretation** — The final CAD model demonstrates:
- Good balance between strength and material efficiency
